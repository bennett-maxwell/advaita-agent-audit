# SKILL-175: Multi-Unit Operator Management
**Version:** 1.0 | **Pillar:** Franchise Lifecycle | **Reversibility:** REVERSIBLE

## PURPOSE
Identify and develop SRP multi-unit operators. High-performers who can expand to 2-5 units.

## TRIGGER
Annual (July) OR franchisee scores > 85 on performance ranking (SKILL-166) for 3 consecutive months.

## EXECUTION STEPS
1. Pull top-performing franchisees from SKILL-166 data
2. Score multi-unit readiness:
   - Current unit performance (top quartile?)
   - Operational sophistication (low support ticket volume)
   - Financial capacity (from SKILL-136 payment history)
   - Interest expressed (GHL notes)
3. Top candidates: generate multi-unit proposal
   - Territory options (from SKILL-168 expansion analysis)
   - Development schedule
   - Multi-unit discount structure (from FDD)
4. MANDATORY: "Any multi-unit expansion requires a new FDD disclosure and agreement."
5. Upload candidate report to Drive: `fki-franchise/multi-unit-candidates-[date].md`
6. Post to #leo-auto: candidate count + top opportunity

## DIAMOND GATE
- T1: FDD required for expansion. No earnings claims ✅
- T2: File > 200 bytes ✅
- T3: Identification and proposal only — Bennett legal gate for offers ✅
