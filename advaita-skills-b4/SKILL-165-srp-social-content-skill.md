# SKILL-165: SRP Social Content Generator
**Version:** 1.0 | **Pillar:** Brand Playbooks | **Reversibility:** REVERSIBLE

## PURPOSE
Generate SRP franchise opportunity content. FDD-compliant messaging. Attracts qualified franchise candidates.

## TRIGGER
Weekly (content calendar) OR Bennett says "SRP social [topic]".

## EXECUTION STEPS
1. Run bennett-intelligence-layer-skill: SRP current positioning
2. Load SRP brand voice + compliance guide: `fki-brand-voice/srp-voice.md`
3. Generate 7 pieces:
   - 3 × IG captions (franchise opportunity / industry / behind scenes)
   - 2 × TikTok scripts (franchise education / sports retail trends)
   - 2 × YouTube Shorts hooks
4. MANDATORY on any financial mention: "This is not a franchise offering. Any franchise offering is made only by a Franchise Disclosure Document."
5. NO earnings claims whatsoever in social content
6. Upload to Drive: `fki-content/srp/social-[week]-[date].md`
7. Post to #leo-auto

## DIAMOND GATE
- T1: FDD disclaimer mandatory on all financial references. Zero earnings claims ✅
- T2: File > 200 bytes ✅
- T3: Draft — no auto-post ✅
