# SKILL-164: SH Social Content Generator
**Version:** 1.0 | **Pillar:** Brand Playbooks | **Reversibility:** REVERSIBLE

## PURPOSE
Generate SH brand social content. Premium positioning. Aspirational tone. Business transformation narrative.

## TRIGGER
Weekly (content calendar) OR Bennett says "SH social [topic]".

## EXECUTION STEPS
1. Run bennett-intelligence-layer-skill: SH current positioning + voice
2. Load SH brand voice guide: `fki-brand-voice/sh-voice.md`
3. Generate 7 pieces:
   - 3 × IG captions (education / transformation / lifestyle)
   - 2 × TikTok scripts (30s — business insight + CTA)
   - 2 × YouTube Shorts hooks
4. FDD disclaimers on any revenue/business performance mentions
5. "Results not typical. Individual results vary."
6. Upload to Drive: `fki-content/sh/social-[week]-[date].md`
7. Post to #leo-auto
