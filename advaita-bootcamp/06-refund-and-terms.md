## Terms of Service (the actual contract, plain English)

**1. What you bought.**
A 7-day micro-engagement to install one AI agent in your business, delivered by Bennett Maxwell or a senior Advaita installer. Three sessions (Session 1: 60 min, Session 2: 45 min, Session 3: 30 min). Plus an async build window of approximately 48 hours between Sessions 1 and 2.

**2. What you didn't buy.**
You didn't buy a course, a SaaS subscription, an ongoing service, or a guarantee of business outcomes. We guarantee the agent runs. We don't guarantee what the agent will earn or save you.

**3. Capacity.**
We deliver 5 Bootcamps per week maximum. If your Session 1 booking is more than 14 days out, that's why. You can request a refund at any time before Session 1 with zero questions.

**4. Your obligations.**
- Show up to your scheduled sessions
- Give us read access to the tools your agent needs (CRM, email, etc.)
- Don't disable the agent in week 1 without telling us

**5. Our obligations.**
- Show up to your scheduled sessions
- Build, deploy, and train you on one (1) AI agent within 7 calendar days
- Maintain confidentiality on anything you share about your business
- Refund your $47 if we don't deliver per the agreement above

**6. Confidentiality.**
We don't share your business details with anyone outside the install team. We may use anonymized aggregated learnings (e.g., "5% of Bootcamp buyers run a law firm") in marketing. We never use named buyer details without explicit written consent.

**7. What happens if you reschedule.**
You can reschedule any session up to 2 hours before it starts via Calendly. If you miss a session with no notice, we'll reach out once to reschedule. Two consecutive no-shows = engagement ends, no refund.

**8. What we don't do.**
- We don't provide tax, legal, or financial advice
- We don't make earnings or savings projections (Results as disclosed in our FDD where applicable. Individual results may vary.)
- We don't take ownership of your data — your CRM, email, and tools stay yours

**9. Disputes.**
First step: reply to bennett@franchiseki.com. We will respond within 2 business days. If we can't resolve in 14 days, mediation in Salt Lake County, Utah (FranchiseKI's home jurisdiction). No class actions.

**10. Changes to terms.**
We may update these terms in the future. Changes apply to future buyers only — you're locked into the terms in force on your purchase date.
